Every Way to Play

Roblox Rivals features multiple structured match queues designed to test different skills—from isolated mechanical isolation to complex multi-target team coordination.

Every Main Gamemode

Mode Name Player Count Victory Condition Primary Tactical Focus
1v1 Duel 2 Players (1 vs 1) First to eliminate the opponent 5 times Raw mechanical skill, aim tracking, and individual positioning.
2v2 4 Players (2 vs 2) First to eliminate the opponent 5 times Team cross-firing, trade eliminations, and callout synergy.
3v3 6 Players (3 vs 3) First to eliminate the opponent 5 times Map control, choke point containment, and structural routing.
Free For All (FFA) 1-40 Players (Solo) Most points achieved within 8 minutes Chaotic target acquisition, fast threat assessment, and survival.
