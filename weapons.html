Weapons in Rivals

In a hitscan shooter where bullet travel time doesn't exist, weapon selection dictates your operational combat distance. Master the frame data, damage thresholds, and specific combat roles for every firearm in the game.

Most Common Primaries

Assault Rifles (The Versatile Meta)

The Assault Rifle stands as the operational baseline for standard loadouts. Balancing a competitive rate of fire with a highly stable recoil recovery profile, it is designed to control mid-lane map sectors. It rewards players with strong track-aiming capabilities and can quickly melt standard hitboxes if you land consecutive headshots.

Sniper (High-Risk Precision)

The Sniper completely shapes the pacing of long-range sightlines. While it penalizes users with slow handling frames and a heavy delay between shots, it counters with massive raw damage. Landing a headshot functions as an instant elimination, making it the premier choice for holding power positions and lockdown sectors.

Burts & Shotguns (Aggressive Flanking)

For high-mobility players who prefer continuous slide-strafing, the Uzi and Shotgun dominate close-range spaces. The Uzi offers the fastest fire rate in the game, letting you overwhelm opponents with pure bullet velocity during mobile fights. The Shotgun, firing in tight pellet spreads, yields extreme burst damage that instantly terminates duels upon a clean, up-close connect.

Top Competitive Loadout Combinations

To win consistently on the ranked ladder, you should avoid bringing overlapping weapons. Try configuring your slots into these optimized competitive archetypes:

  1. The Frontline Duelist: Assault Rifle + Uzi. This setup handles standard mid-range skirmishes while providing a lightning-fast swap option if an opponent slides aggressively into your personal space.
  2. The Map Sentinel: Sniper + Revolver. Ideal for passive anchoring on elevated high ground. The Sniper secures entry picks, while the Pistol offers reliable self-defense if your position gets compromised.
  3. The Aggressive Breacher:Shotgun + Uzi. Built purely for high-speed flanking routing. Use clean movement mechanics to bypass open lanes, closing the distance to force enemy squads into high-panic, close-quarters situations.
